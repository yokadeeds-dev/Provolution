## The Challenge

Global CO₂ emissions: **55 Gt/year**. Current approaches are fragmented, uncoordinated, and insufficient. Climate tipping points approach within decades.

## The Solution: Provolution

A **mathematically rigorous framework** for systematic climate transformation based on the **SEC Principle**:

| Principle | Meaning | Application |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| **S**ufficient | Every measure must achieve its defined effect | No symbolic actions |
| **E**fficient | Minimize resources for maximum impact | Optimal allocation |
| **C**onsistent | No measure may contradict others | System coherence |
