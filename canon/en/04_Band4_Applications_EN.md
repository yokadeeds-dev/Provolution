## CHAPTER 1: INTRODUCTION

### 1.1 What Are Provolution Levers?

Provolution levers are **concrete, quantified measures** for climate transformation that:
- Follow the **SEC Principle** (Sufficient, Efficient, Consistent)
- Are **quantified** (CO₂ impact, resources, time horizon)
- Are validated through **real-world examples** (180+ case studies)
- Are **fully documented** (7 sections per lever)

**Distinction from "Solutions":**
- Not abstract concepts, but implementable systems
- Not isolated measures, but a networked overall system
- Not theoretical models, but validated practices
