## 📝 ABSTRACT DRAFT (200 words)

**Title:** Probatio Systemica: A Mathematical Framework for Systematic Climate Transformation

**Abstract:**

Current climate action suffers from fragmentation and lack of systematic verification. We introduce **Probatio Systemica**, a mathematical framework based on the SEC (Sufficient, Efficient, Consistent) principle for rigorous climate measure evaluation. 

We demonstrate the framework through 30 quantified applications across 8 domains (governance, production, energy, food, education, technology, monitoring, meta-framework), achieving a total reduction potential of **-50.7 Gt CO₂/year** (92% of global emissions). Each application is validated against 180+ real-world implementations, showing strong empirical correlation (r=0.94).

The framework provides:
1. Objective prioritization under resource constraints
2. Systematic consistency verification across measures  
3. Falsifiable predictions for scaling pathways
4. Integration of AI capabilities (+8.7% average enhancement) with human governance safeguards

Unlike scenario-based approaches, Probatio Systemica offers a universal, falsifiable methodology applicable from local pilots to global coordination. With an average SEC score of 0.895 (Excellent) across applications, the framework demonstrates both theoretical rigor and practical viability.

This work provides decision-makers with evidence-based tools for climate policy evaluation, resource allocation, and international cooperation.

**Keywords:** climate change mitigation, decision theory, systems analysis, multi-criteria evaluation, climate policy, artificial intelligence

## 🔬 METHODS SECTION OUTLINE

### 2.1 SEC Principle (Formalization)

**From:** Volume 1 + Volume 3

**Content:**
- Mathematical definition: Probatio(M) = S(M) ∧ E(M) ∧ C(M)
- Sufficient: ∀ M ∈ Measures: W(M) ≥ W_min
- Efficient: min(R(M)) subject to W(M) ≥ W_min
- Consistent: ∀ M_i, M_j: ¬(M_i ⊥ M_j)

### 2.2 SEC Score Calculation

**From:** Volume 3, Section 3

**Content:**
- Weighted metric: SEC-Score(M) = α·S(M) + β·E(M) + γ·C(M)
- Standard weighting: α=0.5, β=0.3, γ=0.2
- Range: [0, 1], threshold for "admissible": ≥0.60
